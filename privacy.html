Privacy policy

Updated 10 October 2026

Your habits stay on your device

HabitMe stores your goals, check-ins, pauses, attempt history, graduation records, and reminder preferences locally on your device. HabitMe has no sign-in account and does not send your habit records to the developer. The app includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, HabitMe schedules reminders and check-in alerts on your device. Notifications may show goal names on your Lock Screen, depending on your iPhone notification settings. You can change permissions in Settings.

Purchases

Apple processes payments for HabitMe Pro. HabitMe uses Apple’s StoreKit service to check whether Pro is unlocked and to restore purchases. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Backups and transfers

You can export a backup containing your goals and history to a location you choose in Files. Exported backups are readable JSON files and are not encrypted by HabitMe. The storage provider you choose may sync them to the cloud. Device backups may also include HabitMe data, depending on your Apple settings. HabitMe does not provide its own cloud sync.

Deleting your information

Deleting a goal removes it from the current goal list and cancels its reminders. A recovery copy saved before your last import may still contain older goals until it is replaced or used. Exported files and device backups are not changed by deleting a goal. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading the app keeps that data. You manage exported files and device backups separately.

Support and privacy questions

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to include and uses it to respond to your request. Please do not send private goal details or backup files unless needed. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations.

Our support website

The public privacy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to provide and protect its hosting service, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies added by HabitMe.

Policy updates

This policy describes the current iPhone version of HabitMe. If its data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.
