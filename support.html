Help & support

Updated 10 October 2026

Move to a new phone

On your old phone, open You → Export backup and save the file somewhere you can access. On your new phone, open You → Import backup. Import replaces the goals already on that phone after you confirm. Undo last import restores the previous local copy.

Restore HabitMe Pro

Open You → Upgrade to Pro (or Manage purchases), then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Restoring Pro does not restore goal data; use your backup for that.

Reminders and check-in alerts

Daily reminders are set individually in Edit goal. Unlogged-day check-in alerts are a separate option in You. Both require notification permission in iPhone Settings. Focus modes and notification settings can affect when you see alerts.

Forgot to log a completed goal?

When a goal is awaiting confirmation, open it and tell HabitMe what happened before the displayed deadline. Confirm only days you actually completed. An unanswered check-in expires; it is different from a planned pause.

Pause or resume

Use Pause when you plan to take a break. You can choose a return date or an open-ended pause, and resume early. Pauses preserve progress. If today is already completed, the pause starts tomorrow.

Delete a goal

Open Edit goal → Delete goal. Cancel keeps the goal. Confirming removes its current progress and history and cancels its reminders. Exported backups are unaffected, and a pre-import recovery copy may still contain the goal.

Contact support

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, HabitMe version, and the steps that caused it. Screenshots can help; hide private goal names and personal information first.
